# ICOR for Life - Canvases 0.2.0

The seven things Tom asked for after testing 0.1.0, plus two live asks
from the same day.

- A click on a connection row opens the canvas at that edge, both cards
  in view. Mod-click opens the note in a new tab.
- The Canvases panel is a section of the core Backlinks pane; the
  separate view and ribbon icon are gone.
- The ink colour and width buttons open flyouts with every option
  visible.
- Select, Hand, Pen and Eraser as one tool group (V, H, P, E), with a
  Group button for two or more selected cards.
- The groups around a card, innermost first, under the canvas name; a
  click opens the canvas at the group.
- "New canvas here" in the background menu, and a breadcrumb inside a
  nested canvas back to each parent.
- Shapes and colours for text cards from the floating toolbar, saved on
  the card's own data.
- The controls column sits on the left by default (setting to put it
  back).

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer; verified on 1.13.7. Copy `main.js`,
`manifest.json` and `styles.css` into
`<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/icor-for-life-canvases/`.
