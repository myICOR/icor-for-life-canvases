# ICOR for Life - Canvases 0.3.0

Tom's second round, five findings on the shapes, and Flint's review of
0.2.0.

- The canvas's card menu sits in the controls column under the tools.
- A zoom bar at the bottom right: fit, minus, the live percentage,
  plus; the percentage opens presets and the minimap toggle.
- A minimap above the zoom bar: click or drag to move, scroll to zoom,
  M to toggle.
- Text colour for text cards, with automatic contrast on a fill; the
  palette is the one outline control; shapes drawn behind the text with
  the text inset and centred; the editor shows the fill through.
- `docs/canvas-format.md` and a coexistence test for other plugins'
  keys.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer; verified on 1.13.7. Copy `main.js`,
`manifest.json` and `styles.css` into
`<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/icor-for-life-canvases/`.
