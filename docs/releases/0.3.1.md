# ICOR for Life - Canvases 0.3.1

The two things the directory's scan of 0.3.0 asked for.

- Shapes are SVG geometry behind the card's content; the stylesheet
  uses no `clip-path`.
- The index finds canvas files through the metadata cache and the
  vault's events; it never enumerates the vault.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer; verified on 1.13.7. Copy `main.js`,
`manifest.json` and `styles.css` into
`<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/icor-for-life-canvases/`.
